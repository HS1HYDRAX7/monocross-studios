Ad embed

Show Monocross Studios banners and popups on your own site. Each slot is one <div>; the script fills it with a live ad or, when no campaign is running, with the banner of an available game. Two kinds: banner and popup. Three sizes: small, medium, big.

Banner · small (320 × 96)

data-monocross-ad="banner" data-size="small"

Banner · medium (320 wide, card)

data-monocross-ad="banner" data-size="medium"

Banner · big (728 wide, leaderboard)

data-monocross-ad="banner" data-size="big"

Popup

data-monocross-ad="popup" data-size="medium" data-delay="3" data-frequency="day"

The popup opens once after the delay. data-frequency="day" shows it once per day, "session" once per browser session. Reload the page to see it again after closing.

Install on your site

<script src="https://monocross-studios.vercel.app/embed.js" async></script>

<div data-monocross-ad="banner" data-size="medium"></div>

<div data-monocross-ad="popup" data-size="big" data-delay="5" data-frequency="day"></div>
